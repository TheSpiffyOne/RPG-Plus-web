How to play RPG Plus

Installation, progression, and settings.

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Fabric

Install the pack.

The Modrinth Launcher is the simplest way to install RPG Plus with its included mods and configuration.

  1. Install the Modrinth Launcher and sign in with the Microsoft account you use for Minecraft: Java Edition.
  2. Search for RPG Plus in the launcher, or open the RPG Plus Modrinth page and install it from there.
  3. Choose the pack version you want to play and let the launcher finish downloading its files.
  4. Launch the RPG Plus instance. Use the pack’s included Minecraft and Fabric versions so its mods stay together.

New to modpacks? You don’t need to download the included mods one by one. The launcher handles the pack as a complete instance.

Prepare your instance.

  • Check your memory allocation. The launcher’s default Java memory limit may be low for a large modpack. Adjust it to suit your available RAM, leaving enough for your operating system and other apps.
  • Give the first load time. Loading the mods and generating terrain with Distant Horizons can take longer than vanilla Minecraft.
  • Look through Controls. Open Minecraft’s Controls settings to find the pack’s skill, map, and quest bindings and resolve any conflicts. Use the bindings shown in your installed version.

Optional quests can provide direction, but you can also ignore the quest log and explore at your own pace.

Survive your first night.

  1. Gather wood, make your first tools, and secure a reliable supply of food.
  2. Set up shelter and light it before pushing into unfamiliar terrain.
  3. Look for equipment upgrades and take manageable fights rather than rushing straight into a dungeon.
  4. Keep track of safe routes and useful places with the pack’s map system.

Grow your character.

RPG Plus adds character levels and skill points. As you progress, spend earned points on skills that fit how you want to play.

  • Find the character or skill menu using the bindings in Controls.
  • Read each skill’s description before spending points, and choose upgrades that complement your equipment and playstyle.
  • Keep looking for new armor and items. Levels and gear work together to open up tougher adventures.

Brave the dungeons.

Prepare food, equipment, and a route back before entering dungeons.

  • Bring food, spare supplies, light sources, and equipment you can rely on.
  • Check your armor and weapons before leaving your base.
  • Keep a route back to safety, and learn from fights that are too difficult for your current build.

Tune the view to your machine.

Distant Horizons and compatible Iris shaders come with the pack. They make a large world feel larger, but the right settings depend on your hardware.

  • If performance is low, check Java memory allocation first and close unnecessary background apps.
  • Lower shader quality and Distant Horizons settings if you need a smoother experience.
  • Allow extra time for terrain generation when exploring a new area.
  • If you install another shader, choose one compatible with Distant Horizons.

Change settings a little at a time so you can tell what helps.

Update with a backup.

  1. Close Minecraft before updating the pack.
  2. Back up your saves and any settings you want to keep.
  3. Read the patch notes for changes and any version-specific instructions.
  4. Update the instance through Modrinth, then check that everything loads correctly before continuing your main world.

Keep the backup. Retain an untouched copy of your world until you’re happy with the updated pack. If a release has special migration steps, follow those first.
